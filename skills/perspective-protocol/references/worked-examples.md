# Worked examples

Derived panels for four kinds of topic. Use them as a pattern for how specific a perspective should be, not as panels to copy.

## Strategy document (a capability maturity model for a B2B services company)

The stock panel fails here. Economist and Historian have little to ask about an internal capability framework, while the perspectives that would actually stress it are absent.

Derived:

- **The client procurement lead**: does this change what I buy, or is it vendor theater?
- **The competitor doing this well**: what does this version miss that ours has?
- **The consultant who has watched maturity models die**: what makes these become shelfware?
- **The account manager**: can I actually use this in a room with a client?
- **The skeptical CEO**: what does this get us that a case study doesn't?

Note that four of five are hostile. That is the point.

## Financial decision (FEIE vs Foreign Tax Credit for a US citizen abroad)

Derived:

- **The US expat CPA**: what does the filing history lock in?
- **The host country's tax authority**: what does the other side of the treaty see?
- **The long-horizon financial planner**: what does this do to the 20-year compounding path?
- **The person who guessed wrong**: what is the failure mode, and is it reversible?

Retrieval is non-negotiable here. Ungrounded tax reasoning is worse than no reasoning, and this is precisely the case where a confident wrong answer costs real money. Treat the output as input to a professional, not a substitute for one.

## Property research (rent-to-own purchase in another country)

Derived:

- **The local lawyer**: what does this contract actually mean in this jurisdiction?
- **The person who has lived there five years**: what does the listing not say?
- **The exit planner**: how do I get out, and what does it cost?
- **The currency and rates view**: what is the macro exposure over the hold period?

## Technical architecture (agent system design)

Derived:

- **The operator at 3am**: what breaks, and can I see why?
- **The cost model**: what does this run at scale?
- **The next maintainer**: can anyone but the author understand this?
- **The adversary**: what is the attack surface?
